Our story

A Sierra Leonean community with global connections.

WiCyS Sierra Leone is an international affiliate of Women in CyberSecurity. Our goal is to make cybersecurity knowledge, belonging and opportunities more accessible across the country.

Meet our team

Our mission

Support women and girls through education, mentorship, technical learning, public digital safety awareness and community collaboration.

Our approach

Start with inclusive and practical programming, create trusted partnerships, and measure real participation and learning outcomes.

What guides our work

Inclusion

Access at every level

Include primary and secondary schools, universities, professionals and local communities.
