What We Do

Programs with Purpose.

Creating opportunities for girls and women at every stage of their cybersecurity journey, from early education to professional leadership.

EDUCATION

School Outreach

Inspiring primary and secondary school students through digital safety, cyber awareness and technology education.

CAMPUS

Campus Connect

Connecting university students through mentorship, campus communities, collaboration and technical activities.

LEARNING

Cyber Skills Academy

Building practical cybersecurity skills through workshops, technical training and structured learning pathways.

COMPETITION

Cyber Challenge

Encouraging creativity, teamwork and problem-solving through school cyber quizzes and university competitions.

COMMUNITY

Mentorship & Community

Bringing together students, mentors and professionals to share knowledge, support growth and build connections.

OPPORTUNITIES

Opportunities Hub

Discover scholarships, cybersecurity training, internships, conferences and professional development resources.
